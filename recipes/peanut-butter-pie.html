Peanut Butter Pie

This peanut butter pie recipe is very easy to make. Garnish with peanut butter cups for an extra special treat!

Prep Time 20 minutes
Additional time 2 hours
Total Time 2 hours 20 mins
Servings 8

Peanut Butter Pie Ingredients

  • Cream cheese: This rich and creamy peanut butter pie starts with a package of cream cheese.
  • Peanut butter: Of course, you’ll need peanut butter! Opt for the smooth kind.
  • Confectioners’ sugar: Make sure you use confectioners’ (a.k.a. powdered) sugar, as it’ll blend smoothly into the filling.
  • ⅓ cup chopped walnuts
  • Frozen whipped topping: A container of frozen whipped topping (such as Cool Whip) is another shortcut ingredient.
  • Graham cracker crust: Use a store-bought or homemade graham cracker crust.
  • Peanut butter cups: Garnish the pie with peanut butter cup candies, such as Reese’s.

    • How to Serve Peanut Butter Pie

    If you don’t want to top this peanut butter pie with whipped topping and peanut butter cups, you can keep things more simple by drizzling each slice with chocolate sauce. Of course, the bare pie will be perfectly delicious as-is.

    How to Store Peanut Butter Pie

    Peanut butter pie should always be stored in the refrigerator and it shouldn’t sit at room temperature for more than two hours at a time. Stored in an airtight container in the fridge, it will last three to four days.

    Can You Freeze Peanut Butter Pie?

    Yes! You can freeze peanut butter pie for about one month, but wait to add the toppings until right before you serve it. Simply wrap the pie in at least one layer of storage wrap, then at least one layer of aluminum foil. Thaw in the refrigerator overnight.

    Ingredients

    • 1 (8 ounce) package cream cheese, softened
    • 1 cup creamy peanut butter
    • 1 cup confectioners' sugar
    • 1 (16 ounce) container frozen whipped topping, thawed, divided
    • 1 (9 inch) prepared graham cracker crust
    • 15 miniature chocolate-covered peanut butter cups (such as Reese's), unwrapped

    Directions

    1. Gather all ingredients.
    2. Mix cream cheese, peanut butter, and confectioners’ sugar together until smooth..
    3. Fold in 1/2 of the whipped topping, then spoon the mixture into graham cracker crust.
    4. Spread remaining whipped topping over the peanut butter mixture and garnish with peanut butter cups. Chill for at least 2 hours or overnight before serving.
    5. Serve and enjoy!
